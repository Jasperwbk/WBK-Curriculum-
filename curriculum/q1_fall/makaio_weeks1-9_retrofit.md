# Makaio — Weeks 1–9 Retrofit

Same pattern: each objective gets 1–2 quick, ungraded check questions, phrased
simpler than Millaray's given his age band.

## Week 1 — Harvest Math & Sorting
**RLA:** 1) 3–5 sentences on a harvest task — Check: "Read me one sentence." 2) Spell 10 harvest words — Check: quiz 2–3 aloud. 3) Read own entry aloud — Check: listen for smoothness.
**Math:** 1) Count/record by type — Check: "How many apples total?" 2) Tally chart by size/color — Check: "Which group has more?" 3) 5 add/subtract word problems — Check: one on the spot.
**Science:** 1) Label root/stem/leaf/fruit/seed — Check: "Point to the root." 2) One sentence per part's job — Check: "What does a leaf do?" 3) Match 3 foods to plant part — Check: "Is a carrot a root or a fruit?"
**Social studies:** 1) One Osage food — Check: "Name it." 2) Point to Webster County — Check: on the map. 3) One similarity to our harvest — Check: "What's alike?"
**Bushcraft:** 1) Recite knife rules — Check: "Say one rule." 2) Safe hand position — Check: watch and confirm. 3) Blood circle — Check: "What is it?"
**Homestead:** 1) 2 steps of water-bath canning — Check: "Name one." 2) Help with a real step — Check: watch. 3) Why we preserve — Check: "Why do we do this?"
**Nature ID:** 1) ID 3 fall plants — Check: "Name one." 2) Safe/not safe — Check: "Is this one safe?" 3) Draw one — Check: show the drawing.
**Spiritual/cultural:** 1) Why give thanks at harvest — Check: "Why do we do this?" 2) One Norse + one Osage/Cherokee tradition — Check: "Name one."

## Note on interleaving
Math and nature ID are his strongest 3-objective subjects for mixed practice
once week 2+ is underway. RLA spelling/reading checks work better as quick
oral spot-checks than written mixed sets at his age.
