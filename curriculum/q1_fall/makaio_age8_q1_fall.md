# Makaio (8 / Rhoe) — Quarter 1: Fall Curriculum

Generic age-8 starting point, re-tune once his real assessment is in. Same weekly
budget as Millaray's (28 hrs: 17 core / 11 specialty) — Missouri's hour law applies
per child regardless of age once past compulsory age 7. Social studies stays a
single local-history strand all quarter (no government/economics track for him yet
— that's Millaray-only for now).

---
## Week 1 — Harvest Math & Sorting

| Subject | Topic | Objectives | Activity | Hrs |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| bushcraft_outdoor_skills | Knife safety intro | 1) Recite the knife safety rules before handling one 2) Demonstrate safe hand position while supervised 3) Explain why the "blood circle" rule matters | Supervised knife safety lesson (no independent use yet) | 3 |
| homestead_skills | Helping can/preserve | 1) Name the 2 main steps of water-bath canning 2) Help with one real step (washing, packing jars) 3) Explain why we preserve food | Assist in a real canning session | 3 |

| spiritual_cultural | Harvest gratitude | 1) Explain in his own words why the family gives thanks at harvest 2) Name one Norse and one Osage/Cherokee harvest tradition touched on 3) Take part in a family gratitude moment | Family harvest gratitude moment | 2 |
