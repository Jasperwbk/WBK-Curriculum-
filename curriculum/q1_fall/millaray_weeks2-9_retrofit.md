# Millaray — Weeks 2–9 Retrofit

Same pattern as Week 1: each objective gets 2 quick, ungraded check questions.
Once a subject has 2+ live objectives, interleave its practice rather than
block by objective.

## Week 2 — Preserving & Food Science
**RLA** (procedural writing)
1. Write a clear step-by-step procedure — Check: "What's step 2 of your recipe?" / "Could I follow this without asking you anything?"
2. Sequencing transition words — Check: "Give me a word that means 'after that.'"
3. Edit a partner's procedure for a missing step — Check: "What step did I skip?" (parent reads a broken procedure aloud)

**Math** (recipe scaling)
1. Scale a recipe using fraction multiplication — Check: "Double 3/4 cup — how much?"
2. Convert cups/tbsp/tsp — Check: "How many tbsp in 1/4 cup?"
3. Real batch-scaling problem — Check: "We have 9 lb of tomatoes, recipe needs 3 lb per batch — how many batches?"

**Science** (preservation chemistry)
1. Why canning/drying/salting prevent spoilage — Check: "Name one thing that causes spoilage."
2. 3 main preservation methods used here — Check: "Name two of the three."
3. Predict unpreserved produce over 2 weeks — Check: "What do you expect to see by day 14?"

**Social studies** (winter-prep history)
1. How families prepared before refrigeration — Check: "Name one pre-electricity method."
2. Compare to our own methods — Check: "Which one do we still use?"
3. One method still used vs. one changed — Check: "What's changed the most?"

**Bushcraft** (fire fundamentals)
1. Build a correct fire lay — Check: "Name the fire lay you used."
2. Fire triangle — Check: "What are the three things fire needs?"
3. Safe extinguishing — Check: "Show me how you'd put this out safely."

**Homestead** (root cellaring)
1. Store-as-is vs. needs-preserving — Check: "Which pile does a potato go in?"
2. Proper storage spot — Check: "Why does this spot work — temp, humidity, or both?"
3. Log expected shelf life — Check: "How long do you expect this to last?"

**Nature ID** (fungi caution)
1. ID 3 fall fungi, safe/unsafe labeled — Check: "Point to one and tell me safe or unsafe."
2. Family rule: never eat unidentified fungi — Check: "What's the rule?"
3. Log with photo/sketch — Check: "Show me today's log entry."

**Spiritual/cultural** (ancestor food traditions)
1. One ancestor/family food tradition — Check: "Name the tradition we recorded."
2. Why preserving mattered across all three honored traditions — Check: "Give one reason."

## Note on interleaving this quarter
Math and science have 3 countable objectives most weeks — best candidates for
mixed-order practice sets. Government/economics (Weeks 5–8) is new territory
for her — keep those checks separate/blocked a bit longer before interleaving
with the local-history track, per the standard's note that a subject can stay
blocked while it's still fragile.
