# Maizley (2.5 / Nova) — Quarter 1: Fall Curriculum

Missouri's 1,000/600/400-hour law does **not** legally apply to Maizley yet
(compulsory attendance starts at 7). This is enrichment, not a legal requirement —
tracked loosely for portfolio consistency and so the ecosystem dashboard has data
on her once she's old enough that it matters. Sessions are short (10–20 min each)
and follow her mood/attention, not a clock. Suggested total: ~5–8 short sessions/
week, no hour target to hit.

Every unit still follows topic → objectives → activity so it's ready to drop into
the same system later, just scaled down: 2 objectives instead of 3–5, and simpler
language.

---
## Week 1 — Harvest Math & Sorting

| Subject | Topic | Objectives | Activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| math | Sorting by color/size | 1) Sort 5+ harvested items by color with help 2) Point to "big" vs. "small" correctly | Sort veggies/fruit into color bowls |
| reading_language_arts | Naming harvest words | 1) Say or attempt 5 harvest-related words (apple, pumpkin, seed) 2) Follow a 1-step instruction ("bring me the red one") | Name-and-fetch game during harvest |
| nature_identification | Touch & texture | 1) Describe (or point to) rough/smooth/bumpy textures on 3 harvested items | Texture exploration table |

## Week 2 — Preserving & Food Science

| Subject | Topic | Objectives | Activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| math | Big/little jars | 1) Match same-size lids to jars 2) Count to 3 while helping hand over items | Jar/lid matching during canning prep |
| science | Smell & taste (safe items only) | 1) Identify 2 smells as "yummy" or "not yummy" 2) Watch and imitate one simple kitchen motion (stirring) | Sensory kitchen helper time |

## Week 3 — Seed Saving & Plant Life Cycles

| Subject | Topic | Objectives | Activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| math | Seed counting | 1) Count to 5 with help while dropping seeds into a cup 2) Copy a simple pattern (seed, seed, gap) | Seed-dropping counting game |
| science | Seed to sprout | 1) Point to "seed" and "plant" pictures correctly 2) Help pat soil over a planted seed | Plant one big easy seed (bean) together |

## Week 4 — Signs of Fall / Nature Study

| Subject | Topic | Objectives | Activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| reading_language_arts | Leaf words | 1) Say or point to "leaf," "tree," "wind" on request 2) Follow a 2-step instruction ("pick it up, put it here") | Leaf collecting walk |
| nature_identification | Color matching | 1) Match a fall leaf to its color on a color card 2) Point out 1 animal or bird seen outside | Leaf color-matching activity |

## Week 5 — Animal Signs & Tracking

| Subject | Topic | Objectives | Activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| science | Animal sounds/names | 1) Make or attempt 3 animal sounds correctly matched to the animal 2) Point to a track shape that matches a picture card | Animal sound + track picture matching |
| math | Baby maze / simple path | 1) Complete a simple large-print maze with one turn, with help 2) Follow a "follow the path" physical game outside | Simple baby maze worksheet + outdoor path-following game |

## Week 6 — How Things Work (Mechanical Reasoning)

| Subject | Topic | Objectives | Activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| science | Turning & opening | 1) Turn a knob/latch with help and understand "open/closed" 2) Watch a simple tool in use and point to it when named | Supervised latch/knob play with real coop latch |
| math | Shape sorting | 1) Correctly place 3+ shapes into a shape sorter 2) Point to "round" vs. "square" on request | Shape sorter play |

## Week 7 — Weather & Data

| Subject | Topic | Objectives | Activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| science | Weather words | 1) Point to or say "sunny," "cloudy," "windy" correctly when shown a picture or looking outside 2) Feel and react to "warm" vs. "cool" air | Daily "what's the weather" look-outside routine |
| reading_language_arts | Weather picture book | 1) Sit through a short weather-themed picture book 2) Point to 2 named objects in the pictures | Read-aloud + point-and-name |

## Week 8 — How People Prepared for Winter (History)

| Subject | Topic | Objectives | Activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| reading_language_arts | Story time | 1) Sit through a short family/winter-prep themed story 2) Point to or name 1 character or object from the story | Family story time |
| homestead_skills | Helper tasks | 1) Carry or hand over 1 safe item during a real winter-prep task 2) Imitate 1 adult action (stacking, sorting) | Supervised "helper" time during real winter prep |

## Week 9 — Winter Nights: Review, Portfolio, and Blót

| Subject | Topic | Objectives | Activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| reading_language_arts | Show & tell | 1) Point to or name her favorite thing from the quarter (a leaf, a jar, a picture) 2) Sit with the family during the Winter Nights observance | Simple show-and-tell with a parent |
| spiritual_cultural | Winter Nights participation | 1) Participate in 1 small part of the family observance (lighting a candle with help, placing an item) | Family Winter Nights observance, toddler-scaled |

---
## Notes
- No hour targets — this is enrichment, logged for consistency only, not for MO
  compliance.
- Objectives are intentionally soft ("with help," "or point to") since success at
  this age is participation and repeated exposure, not mastery — re-tune freely as
  she shows readiness for more or less.
- Once real assessment-style observation is possible (per the toddler assessment
  design already agreed — following instructions, shape/color, simple baby-maze
  critical thinking), these objectives can be tightened against her actual starting
  point the same way Millaray's and Makaio's will be.
