# Millaray — Week 1 Retrofit (pilot for the v2 standard)

Each objective now carries 2 quick check questions — short, ungraded,
answerable in the moment, not a redo of the placement test. Once a subject
has 2+ objectives this week, its practice set should interleave them (mix
order) rather than do all of Objective 1's checks before Objective 2's.

## reading_language_arts — Harvest journaling
1. Write a structured 3-paragraph descriptive entry on a harvest task
   - Check: "Name one sensory detail you could add to a harvest sentence."
   - Check: "Read your paragraph back — does it have a beginning, middle, end?"
2. Use 5+ sensory/descriptive words correctly
   - Check: "Give me a word for how a tomato feels, not just looks."
   - Check: Point to one descriptive word in today's writing and say what sense it's for.
3. Correctly punctuate compound sentences
   - Check: "I picked tomatoes ___ I sorted them by size." (fill the joiner + comma)
   - Check: Find one compound sentence in your own paragraph.

## math — Harvest measurement & value
1. Weigh/record produce to the nearest oz/lb
   - Check: "This bowl weighs 2 lb 4 oz — how many total ounces?"
   - Check: Weigh one real item, record it correctly.
2. Calculate price-per-lb and total value
   - Check: "5 lb at $2/lb — total?"
   - Check: "$15 for 5 lb — what's the price per lb?"
3. Convert oz↔lb at 90%+ accuracy
   - Check: "32 oz = how many lb?"
   - Check: "2.5 lb = how many oz?"

## science — Plant biology of harvest crops
1. Label the harvested part on 5 plants
   - Check: "A carrot — root, stem, or fruit?"
   - Check: "A pumpkin holds seeds — what part is it?"
2. Explain why the plant produces that part
   - Check: "Why does a plant make fruit, in one sentence?"
3. Diagram a seed-to-harvest life cycle
   - Check: "What comes right after 'seed' in the cycle?"

## social_studies_history — Osage foodways & local harvest history
1. Describe 2 Osage food-gathering/preservation practices
   - Check: "Name one thing the Osage grew or gathered."
2. Compare to our homestead harvest
   - Check: "What's one way our harvest is similar to theirs?"
3. Locate Osage range vs. Webster County on a map
   - Check: Point to Webster County without looking at the label.

## bushcraft_outdoor_skills — Knife safety & cordage prep
1. Demonstrate safe knife handling incl. blood circle rule
   - Check: "What is the blood circle?"
2. Process a natural fiber into cordage
   - Check: "What plant part did you use for cordage today?"
3. State 2 survival uses for cordage
   - Check: "Name one thing cordage is used for in a survival kit."

## homestead_skills — Water-bath canning basics
1. Explain water-bath vs. pressure canning, when each applies
   - Check: "Is jam high-acid or low-acid? Which method does it need?"
2. Describe every step of a canning session
   - Check: "What's the step right after packing the jars?"
3. Identify 2 signs of improper seal/spoilage
   - Check: "Lid popped up — good seal or bad?"

## nature_identification — Fall plant ID
1. Identify 5 fruiting/seeding plants on the property
   - Check: Point to and name one plant without prompting.
2. Distinguish one safe-to-eat plant from a dangerous look-alike
   - Check: "What's the one key difference between [pair] you found today?"
3. Log IDs with location notes
   - Check: Read back one log entry — does it have a location noted?

## spiritual_cultural — Hringvegr harvest blót
1. Explain the harvest blót's place in the calendar
   - Check: "What season does the harvest blót mark?"
2. Compare to Osage/Cherokee harvest-gratitude practices
   - Check: "Name one thing the traditions we've looked at have in common."
