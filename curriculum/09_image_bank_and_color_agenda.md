# Image bank + Daily Color Sheet Agenda

Color sheets are **not** random fall clip-art. Full assignment model: [`10_daily_color_sheet_model.md`](10_daily_color_sheet_model.md).

Short version:
- Each kid, each school day: **one unique subject** + **one color sheet of that subject**
- Complexity follows **age / art-ability band** (toddler / 8 / 10) until art assessments upload
- The three kids **never** get the same subject or the same picture that day

Agenda header (pledge, date, "today I…") can be a shared layout. The **drawing is unique**.

---

## License note
Photo folder includes web-search references. Some have stock watermarks. Do **not** print watermarked photos as the color sheet. Color sheets are generated line art (see examples). Government charts are safer to reprint on *study* pages.

---

## Agenda chrome (shared layout, unique drawing)

Letter paper, physical, printed with that day's featured-subject packet:

1. Header: kid name + date + weekday + season
2. Pledge checkbox
3. Q1 week theme one-liner
4. **Featured subject name** (the one this kid uniquely owns today)
5. Color sheet occupying most of the page (band-appropriate, subject-matched)
6. Weather circle + optional temperature
7. Hours ticks (Millaray / Makaio only)
8. "Today I …" line

Toddler: skip hour ticks; bigger drawing.
