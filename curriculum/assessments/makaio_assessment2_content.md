## Answer Key & Parent Notes — Makaio
*(Parent-only page. Nothing here is graded — a guide for reading results.)*

**Reading & Writing** — No fixed answer. Note reading fluency level and
whether written sentences show clear, connected thoughts. Look for: does
the retelling capture the gist, or just fragments?

**Math Trail — Correct Answers**
| Problem | Answer |
|---|---|
| 7 + 8 | 15 |
| 23 − 9 | 14 |
| 6 × 4 | 24 |
| $20 − $12 | $8 |

**History, Science, Builder's Challenge, Outdoor Know-How** — Open-ended by
design, no single right answer. Look for: can they explain their reasoning
out loud, not just state a fact? For the bucket puzzle, one valid path: fill
the 3-gallon, pour into the 5-gallon, fill the 3-gallon again, pour into the
5-gallon until full (uses 1 gallon), leaving 2 gallons in the 3-gallon —
empty the 5-gallon, pour that 2 gallons in, then top off with the 3-gallon.
Alternatively: fill the 5-gallon, pour off into the 3-gallon (2 gallons
remain in the 5-gallon). Either is a valid partial path worth discussing.
