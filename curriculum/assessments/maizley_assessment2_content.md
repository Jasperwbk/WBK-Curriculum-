Not a test — just play with a purpose! Try this over a few short moments
today and check off what she can do.

## Following Instructions
- [ ] Follows a one-step instruction (sit down, give it to me)
- [ ] Follows a two-step instruction (pick up the toy, put it in the box)
- [ ] Points to a named body part

## Shapes & Colors
- [ ] Recognizes circle, square, and triangle
- [ ] Recognizes at least 3 colors
- [ ] Can match same shapes or colors together

## Little Puzzle
Try a simple maze, shape sorter, or stacking cups.
- [ ] Completed with no help
- [ ] Completed with a little guidance
- [ ] Needed hands-on help

---
## Parent Notes — Maizley
There's no pass/fail here — this is a snapshot, not a test. Use it to
notice what she's already doing well and what to gently introduce more of.

**What to Look For**
- Following instructions: mostly about attention and language comprehension
  at this age, not obedience — a "no" or ignoring is normal, not a red flag
  on its own.
- Shapes & colors: recognizing 2–3 and matching some is a solid starting
  point at this age; more is a bonus, not an expectation.
- Puzzle: the goal is watching problem-solving persistence, not speed or
  independence.
